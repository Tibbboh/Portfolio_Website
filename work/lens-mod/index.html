Drawing
Minolta_Hi-matic_Lens adapter

Two vintage lens elements, from a Minolta Hi-matic and an L35AF, adapted to shoot on a Sony α7R or other modern digital cameras. L35AF adapter (Ø41.9 × 6.0 mm). Hi-matic adapter (Ø56.5 × 16.0 mm, M42×1 thread). The L35AF adapter was printed three times, adjusting the thread offset by observing play. Adjustments incrementally changed until it threaded cleanly into the lens part. Those measurements were then used to print the Hi-matic adapter.
