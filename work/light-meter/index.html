//Light Meter

/ Hardware/ Electronics

A handheld meter that turns measured light into aperture and shutter pairs for use with medium format cameras.

A handheld light meter that turns measured light into aperture and shutter-speed pairs for shooting film. It’s built on an Arduino Nano with a BH1750 lux sensor and an ST7789 display. I went through three housings. The first wrapped around the camera, designed from a 3D scan of the body. I then moved to a standalone handheld unit. My understanding of LiPos was rudimentary at this stage, and the prior version ran off a somewhat rigged portable charger. The next iteration should carry a smaller rechargeable battery unit.
