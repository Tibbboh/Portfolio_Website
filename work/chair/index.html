//Chair Study

/ Metal/ Build

A steel and plywood chair designed to house multiple positions and functions in a single form.

Status
Complete
Materials
Cold-rolled steel, plywood
Process
Rhino, MIG welding, woodworking
Positions
Low seat, high seat, bench

A steel and plywood chair, designed by testing seating angles directly against how the body sits. It works in three positions: low seat, high seat and bench. I modeled it in Rhino and built it with MIG-welded cold-rolled steel and plywood.
