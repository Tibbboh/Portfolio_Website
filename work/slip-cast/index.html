//Print to Slip Cast

/ Material/ Fabrication

Testing a direct path from 3D printing to slip casting, with no extra processing in between.

Status
Complete
Process
Rhino model, low-poly mesh, 3D-printed master, two-part plaster mold, slip casting
Finding
Too rigid, too many seams to skip finishing

A test of whether a 3D print can go straight to slip casting with no extra processing. The steps were a Rhino model, a low-poly mesh, a 3D-printed master, a two-part plaster mold, and then the casts.
