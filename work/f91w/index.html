A redesigned housing for the Casio F-91W that keeps the stock internals but makes the body slimmer (33.2 × 38.4 × 7.0 mm). Five prototypes have been printed so far, alongside a two-sheet technical drawing. The finished housing is intended to be metal. Iterations have primarily sought to reduce height and edges to make the watch better suited to outdoor activity.
