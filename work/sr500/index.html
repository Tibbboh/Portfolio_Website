An ongoing repair of a 1978 single-cylinder Yamaha SR500, fixing faults as they show up. So far I’ve cleaned the carburetor and traced an oil leak to a stripped bolt hole, which I re-threaded with a 1/4"-20 Helicoil. A small breakthrough beside the repaired hole still needs to be sealed before the leak is fully fixed. I’ve also tensioned the chain and have ridden a collective 2,000 miles.
