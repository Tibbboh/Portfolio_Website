An ongoing repair of a 1978 single-cylinder Yamaha SR500, fixing faults as they show up. The first teardown covered an air filter clean, a battery recharge and carburetor troubleshooting, which turned up a clogged pilot jet and main jet. An oil leak traced to a stripped bolt hole, which I re-tapped for a 1/4"-20 Helicoil. The first attempt at sealing the breakthrough beside it failed, and oil now leaks through the threads. I’ve also tensioned the chain and have ridden a collective 2,000 miles.
