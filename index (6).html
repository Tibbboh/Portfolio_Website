Hand-built coil vessels in layered mixed slip, paired with a Grasshopper script that tries to describe the rules that made them. The script starts from a base circle and stacks it into layers. It divides each layer into points, pushes them out with a bulge wave, then lofts and smooths the result into a mesh. Seed pods and pollen surfaces were the pattern references, and the script produces a range of digital twins of the physical pieces.
