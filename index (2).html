//SHED

/ Space/ Hardware/ Build

An abandoned garden shed retrofitted with custom hardware and dynamic apertures.

Status
Complete
Context
Senior work
Site
Bennington College, south campus
Materials
Reclaimed lumber, site-milled wood, steel, cable, brick
Tools
Rhino, pen drawing, wood and metal shop
Mentor
Donald Sherefkin

A restored abandoned garden shed on the south side of campus, rebuilt as a space for students to isolate and observe the surrounding landscape. The redesign features retrofit apertures and hardware. The structure is unified by a pulley system that allows two apertures to open in response to one another. The rebuild used reclaimed lumber from the site, and the landscape was surveyed and documented, including the species distribution. Design objects were drawn in Rhino and fabricated in the metal shop.

Process

  1. Survey to drawing

  2. Custom aperture and lock hardware

  3. Steel pulley

  4. Testing the mechanism at scale

  5. Installed
